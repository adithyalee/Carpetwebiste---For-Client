    Do you match or beat other carpet prices?

    Yes. Send us a written quote for the same product or job and we will match it or beat it.

    What is the difference between open stairs and closed stairs?

    Closed stairs have a riser board between treads, so carpet covers both tread and riser. Open stairs have gaps between treads, so carpet covers the treads only.

    Do you install carpet tiles and laminate?

    Yes. We supply and fit carpet, carpet tiles and laminate for homes and businesses.

    Can you repair my existing carpet?

    Yes. We repair burns, tears and loose areas, and re stretch carpet that has rippled.
